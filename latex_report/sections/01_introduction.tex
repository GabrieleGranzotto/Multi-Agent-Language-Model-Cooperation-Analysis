\section{Introduction \& Research Question}
In this work, we explore the role of \textbf{natural-language communication} in strategic multi-agent interactions, specifically focusing on how pre-play communication can influence \textbf{collective outcomes} and \textbf{defection incentives}.
We investigate whether allowing agents to engage in non-binding communication, often referred to as \textbf{"cheap talk"}, can lead to emergent cooperation in scenarios where agents might otherwise defect.
